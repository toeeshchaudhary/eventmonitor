EVENT MONITORING NOTICE
=======================

This machine is owned and operated by the event organisers. It is monitored.

WHAT IS RECORDED
  - which application and window is in the foreground, and when
  - when applications start and stop
  - periodic screenshots of the screen
  - typing ACTIVITY (how much you type, and in which window)

BY DEFAULT, WHAT YOU TYPE IS NOT RECORDED AS TEXT.
If the organisers have enabled character capture for a specific task, that
task's instructions will say so explicitly, on paper, before you start.

NOT RECORDED
  Windows matching the exclusion list (password managers, banking, sign-in
  screens) are suppressed: no screenshots, no typing activity, no content.

WHAT WE DO WITH IT
  Used only to verify that submitted work was produced by the person who
  submitted it, and to resolve disputes during the event. Not used for
  advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone outside the organising team.

RETENTION
  Deleted automatically after the retention window configured for this event.
  Deleting on request: speak to the organiser on duty.

SENSITIVE INPUT
  Do not type personal passwords or banking details on this machine.
  Ask staff for an unmonitored device or your own phone if you need to.

CONSENT
  By using this machine you acknowledge the above. You may decline: ask the
  organiser on duty and an unmonitored device will be provided. Declining
  does not affect your participation in the event.
